# Projects

## EcoQuest

EcoQuest is a Flutter/Dart educational application designed to help primary school students explore flora and fauna in outdoor environments.

Technologies:
- Flutter
- Dart
- TensorFlow Lite
- Gemini
- Supabase
- Google Sign-In
- AR

Key features:
- Offline species recognition
- AI-powered learning prompts
- AI question and answer functionality
- Species information
- AR species details
- Learning quests
- Journaling
- Collections
- Quizzes
- XP and badges
- Daily and weekly activities
- Leaderboard
- QR functionality
- Supabase synchronization

The application uses a TensorFlow Lite model for offline species recognition. Gemini is used for learning prompts and question-answer functionality.

The project supports SDG 4: Quality Education and SDG 15: Life on Land.
